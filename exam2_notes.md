## How to study

1. **Re-do the activities cold** (Days 9–21), then compare to the posted
   solutions. Then Problem Sets 3–5 the same way.
2. **Know which way things move.** "As the model gets more flexible, training
   error ___, test error ___, bias ___, variance ___." Getting these backwards
   was last year's most common mistake.
3. **For each method, finish:** "I use this when ..." / "I choose its tuning
   parameter by ..." / "People mix it up with ...".
4. **Explain your choices out loud.** That's what the short answers ask for.

Two or three sentences per short answer is plenty — answer what was asked, and
be specific.
